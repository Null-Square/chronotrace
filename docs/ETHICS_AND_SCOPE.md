# Ethics and Scope

## Intended use

ChronoTrace is intended for:

- controlled scientific research;
- authorized model auditing;
- internal training-pipeline governance;
- reproducibility analysis;
- unlearning and intervention verification;
- research on capability provenance.

## Data policy

Use synthetic, licensed, public, or organization-owned data for project validation.

Do not place classified, stolen, confidential third-party, or unlawfully obtained material in the repository or public experiment artifacts.

## Interpretation policy

A forensic score is evidence under a stated experimental model. It is not automatic proof of:

- data theft;
- copyright infringement;
- classified-data use;
- contractual breach;
- model ownership.

Real-world training pipelines have hidden confounders. Public claims require calibrated uncertainty and alternative explanations.

## Dual-use consideration

Training-history inference can improve accountability, but it can also reveal information about proprietary pipelines. The public project should prioritize scientific benchmarks and defensive auditing. Higher-risk capabilities should receive a separate release review before publication.

## Human claims

Do not identify or accuse a person or organization based only on ChronoTrace output. High-impact conclusions require independent evidence and appropriate legal or governance review.
