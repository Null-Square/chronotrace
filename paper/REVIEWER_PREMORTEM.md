# Current reviewer risks

The article's main risks are the limited small-model and head-only scope, known-recipe/global-regularity assumptions, classical component proof techniques, factorial middle matching, the untimed oracle-scored ranked baseline, twelve sampled primary permutations on one dataset, lack of formal floating-point bounds, and absence of external replication. The strong-FP16 failure is retained rather than minimized.

The paper explicitly states these boundaries. Success against complete replay is not superiority over all temporal inference methods. For supported evidence see `../docs/PUBLICATION_EVIDENCE.md`; for submission gates see `SUBMISSION_CHECKLIST.md`.
